# Inventory Business Overview

## Business Objective

Inventory exists to ensure that customers can purchase products that are genuinely available while protecting the platform from overselling and avoidable order cancellations.

## Customer Impact

Incorrect inventory data can cause:

- A customer to purchase an unavailable product
- Cancellation after payment
- Delayed refunds
- Incorrect delivery promises
- Loss of customer trust

## Business Impact

Inventory failures can lead to:

- Lost revenue
- Increased customer-service contacts
- Refund and compensation costs
- Fulfillment inefficiency
- Reduced conversion and retention

## Key Quality Principle

Inventory must be accurate, timely, consistent, and safely updated across all participating systems.
